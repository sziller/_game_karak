1. Variables

  • All players have total_of_hp = 5 they start with.

2. Turn

  • Every Player has total_of_actions = 4 Actions in a Turn.

  • After the final Action() in a given Turn the active Player may - in some cases: must - perform one or more FreeActions.

  • Turn is always owned by exactly one Player, but other Players may still be affected by events during that Turn.

  • Based on the game’s ruleset: Turns are strictly Player influenced sequences. (mostly active player guided, but in the case of PvP fight, inactive Players can block the flow) A Turn can only - from the games perspective - end as a direct consequence of a Players action.

  • In later stages however we want to introduce timers to avoid lag/drop/slow reaction block the game. Timer would apply for a given Turn and end a Players Turn it time_limit_turn = 45 seconds.

  • For undetermined Turn endings, we introduce an |End Turn| button on the UI / API / Engine’s logic. This requires the Turn-owners engagement with a button, thus a Turn can only end 2 GameLogic based ways: (either - or)

    • Player performs a TurnEndingFreeAction

    • Player presses the |End Turn| button.

    • …​or - optionally - force closed by hitting time_limit_turn and default to predetermined outcomes.
      (such determined events will prefer default behavior: pick up item if space in inventory, heal if on fountain)

  • For foreign Player based interaction, we will introduce a local timer (e.g.: time_limit_steal for looting defeated active Player)

Building blocks

Turn is made up of the below ingredients, all triggered by Active Player interaction:

2.1. Action()

price: int = 1
does_end_turn = False

Definition

Consumes 1 Action from the active Player’s nr_of_actions_left Turn.

Types

Move()

Move between 2 adjacent discovered Tiles.

Blink()

A normal Move between 2 adjacent discovered Tiles, disregarding pathing restrictions. Enabled by skill_wiz_02

Teleport()

Relocation by teleport effect.

Included variants:

  • between teleport gates

  • onto a specific Player

  • swapping place with a specific Player

  • onto a Monster

Peek()

Reveal a HiddenTile without moving into it.

Process

  • determine location of the Tile: place()

  • take the upper Tile from the tile_pool

  • rotate it: lock()

  • if type == room, populate() it

  • target Tile becomes status = discovered

  • target Tile becomes visited = False

Notes

  • visited state matters for Arena Tiles

  • Peek() must be announced in advance - requires: > skill_ran_02 <

  • later movement into this now-discovered Tile is a separate Action

Discover()

Move from a discovered Tile into a HiddenSpace.

Process

  • perform Peek as part of this Action

  • then move into the Tile

  • target Tile becomes discovered

  • target Tile becomes visited

Pull()

Special Action related to <skill_sco_02>.

This violates the otherwise fully disclosed public game concept. For the same of simplicity, we may only enforce this on UI lvl - in the first iteration.

Process

  • take the upper Tile from the TilePool

  • do not reveal it to the others

  • store it privately

Restrictions and notes

  • the Player does not move into it

  • it has not yet become discovered

  • this must be announced in advance

  • later Discover of this stored Tile is a separate Action

  • <skill_sco_02> owner needs private stored-Tile inventory slots

  • stored Tile capacity: T = 3

Discover Stored

Same as Discover, but instead of taking the upper Tile from the TilePool, the Player uses an arbitrary stored Tile from his own private inventory.

2.2. FreeAction()

price: int = 0
do_end_turn = False

Definition

Does not consume an Action point.

Types

VampireScroll

The Player who activates the Thorn of Darkness spell scroll chooses another hero and steals 1 HP from him.

Effect

  • target hero loses 1 HP

  • activating hero gains 1 HP

Restrictions

  • it can only be activated during the activating Player’s own Turn

  • it does not consume an Action

  • it does not end the Turn

  • it is single-use, then removed from the game

  • multiple Thorns of Darkness may be used in one Turn

  • they may even target the same hero repeatedly

HealingPortalScroll

May be used on self or on any hero during the active Player’s Turn.

Effect

  • target hero is moved to any revealed Healing Fountain

  • target hero is fully healed

  • any curse is removed from the target

Restrictions

  • does not consume an Action

  • does not end the Turn

Combat

Always followed by ItemPickUp()

ItemRearrange

Ease-of-life feature, not core gameplay, but still backend-relevant.

Players may rearrange Items inside their Inventory as long as the final state of Items on the ground does not change.

Purpose

  • allows inventory reordering

  • compensates for the fact that rearrangement of a full Inventory category may otherwise require illegal temporary use of ground slots

2.3. TurnEndingFreeAction()

price: int = 0
do_end_turn = True

Definition

A subclass of FreeAction. After being performed, it ends the current Player’s Turn.

Types

Healing()

Only possible if the Player is on a Healing Fountain.

Effect

  • Player is healed to full health

  • if cursed, the curse is removed

Refer to

  • bar_skill_01 may decide the resulting HP amount after healing instead of forced full heal

Retreat

After a Monster fight, in case of a loss-outcome or tie-outcome.

Effect

  • Player is moved back to the LastValidTile visited during the current Turn

Refer to

  • bat_skill_01

    • may be teleported back to where he came from

    • may choose an adjacent ValidTile

  • wiz_skill_02

    • may move back across a wall

ItemPickUp
Details

  • automatically follows a Fight, even if no actual Item changes hands

  • also used when picking up an Item from the ground, left there earlier by a Player

Effect

  • Player resolves his Item pickup opportunity

Canonical rule

  • ItemPickUp is a TurnEndingFreeAction

Consequences

  • by default, once ItemPickUp is resolved, the current Turn ends

  • this remains true even if no Item is actually moved

2.4. Fixed process: Fight()

price: int = 0
do_end_turn = None

Definition

Determined Action progression. Entering a Field with a Monster in it results in a Fight.

Exceptions

  • pri_skill_02

  • thi_skill_02

Also, first time entering an Arena means the plyaer challenges an other Player to a fight.

Fight trigger sources

This can happen after

  • Move

  • Blink

  • Teleporting: <bat_skill_02>

  • Discover

  • first-time entry into an Arena

    • Combat exception: entering Player is attacker

  • being summoned into an Arena

    • this is an OffTurnEvent

    • no Action point is required for summoning or being summoned

Fight structure

After the initial relocation, which must already have decreased Players nr_of_actions by one, a Fight consists of:

  • Combat()

    • resolve outcome

    • final dice arrangement may be stored for skill_swo_02

    • outcome is determined - determine_outcome() → str: ["win", "tie", "loss"]

  • ItemPickUp()

    • automatically follows Combat

    • happens even if no actual Item is moved

    • canonically produces Turn end

Fight end sequence

Canonical sequence

Combat → ItemPickUp → PendingTurnEnd(cause=ITEM_PICKUP) → hook-based rule checks run → either the Turn ends, or the pending Turn-end is canceled

Important

  • there is no alternative core sequence such as Combat → ItemPickUpNotEnding

  • ItemPickUp always remains canonically Turn-ending

  • any exception acts on the consequence of ItemPickUp, not on the definition of ItemPickUp

2.5. TurnEndingFreeEvent()

Definition

Automatic events, triggered by player actvity

Types

Knock-out()

Once a Players HP reaches 0, Player looses conciousness and his Turn ends

Hook
= skill_wrr_02 =
Recovery()

At Turn start, if a Players HP is exacly 0: - player’s HP is set to exactly 1 - Healing() is ran

Turn-end bridge

Some rules may override the Turn-ending consequence of an otherwise Turn-ending event.

This does not redefine the event itself.

Instead:

  • the event is resolved normally

  • it creates a pending Turn-end consequence

  • then a rule-specific bridge may cancel that consequence

This is the canonical interpretation for skill_swo_02.

3. Skill interaction layers around ItemPickUp

3.1. skill_bar_02

skill_bar_02 acts as a pre-event bypass.

It may skip the ItemPickUp event itself.

Consequences

  • if ItemPickUp is skipped, then ItemPickUp does not end the Turn

  • this does not weaken the base rule, because the event was not performed

3.2. skill_swo_02

skill_swo_02 acts as a post-event bridge.

It does not change the nature of ItemPickUp.

Instead:

  • ItemPickUp resolves normally

  • it creates a pending Turn-end consequence

  • then skill_swo_02 may cancel that pending Turn-end, if its conditions are fulfilled

Thus, there is no separate ItemPickUpNotEnding event.

3.3. OffTurnEvent

Definition

Backend/runtime event happening outside the affected Player’s own Turn.

Examples

  • being challenged to an Arena fight

    • changes location of Player

    • forces him to fight as defender

  • being relocated by hostile Teleport Swap

    • changes location of Player

  • HP change by outside actor

    • healed by bea_skill_02: +1 HP

    • injured by VampireScroll: -1 HP

4. Hook-style skill application

The backend may apply skills through hook points.

Recommended hook points around Fight resolution

  • before_combat

  • after_combat

  • before_itempickup

  • after_itempickup

  • before_turn_end_commit

  • after_turn_end_commit

4.1. Recommended skill placement

  • skill_bar_02

    • hooks into before_itempickup

    • may skip the ItemPickUp event

  • skill_swo_02

    • hooks into before_turn_end_commit

    • may cancel the pending Turn-end caused by ItemPickUp, if all conditions are fulfilled

5. skill_swo_02 precise interpretation

skill_swo_02 does not create an alternative Fight sequence.

It does not replace:

Combat → ItemPickUpTurnEnding

with:

Combat → ItemPickUpNotEnding

Instead, the sequence stays:

Combat → ItemPickUp → PendingTurnEnd(cause=ITEM_PICKUP)

Then, if the conditions of skill_swo_02 are fulfilled, the relevant hook may cancel that pending Turn-end.

Typical conditions may include

  • it is still that Player’s own Turn

  • the Player still has Action capacity to continue

  • the final stored dice arrangement contains at least one 6

6. Healing and HP timing notes

`OffTurnEvent`s may alter HP outside the affected Player’s own Turn.

Examples

  • bea_skill_02 may heal a Player

  • VampireScroll may injure a Player

  • hostile relocation may move a Player onto a Healing Fountain

Therefor Turn-start and Turn-end healing rules must remain invariant-based and not depend on HP changing only inside one’s own Turn.

7. Summary invariant

ItemPickUp is always canonically Turn-ending.

Exceptions are handled in two different ways

  • by skipping the event itself: skill_bar_02

  • by canceling the pending Turn-end consequence after the event resolved: skill_swo_02

8. Further concepts and rules

8.1. Tiles

Different states of a given Tile:

  • discovered: <bool>

    • False: unplaced but already drawn tiles

    • True: means the Tile is already on the Field (location and rotation determined), it also has or has had a monster/ item in it, if type == room visited: <bool>

      • False: as a result of Peek() the Tile is fully set and eventually also populated, but has never been entered. A Tile archetype_id = "room_x_arena" is activates a PvP Fight on turning from False → True

      • True: at least one Player has already occupied the tiles location before, or is now.

8.2. ValidTile / InvalidTile

InvalidTile

  • Tiles with a living Monster in them are InvalidTiles

  • You cannot end (thus start) your Turn on an Invalid Tile

ValidTile

  • not InvalidTile’s are ValidTiles

  • you my end your turn on ValidTiles, thus you may also retreat to such Tiles

8.3. Movement restriction from monster Tiles

A Player may not move from a Monster-occupied Tile.

Exceptions

  • pri_skill_02

  • thi_skill_02

Even these exceptions do not allow a Player to end his Turn on an InvalidTile.

Therefore, in case of loss-outcome or tie-outcome, such Players are moved back to their current Turn’s LastValidTile.

8.4. LastValidTile

LastValidTile must be tracked Turn-by-Turn for each Player.

Rules

  • it stores the last ValidTile visited by the Player during the current Turn

  • it is guaranteed to reset at Turn end

  • it is used by Retreat and related fallback mechanics
